Hi! I'm Diana, a third-year Electrical Engineering + Design student at Stanford University, I'm fascinated by how electrical systems come together in complex, multidisciplinary projects. My experiences have ranged from designing avionics and electrical systems for rockets to testing sensor systems for particle detectors and HVAC applications. Along the way, I've become especially interested in semiconductor technologies, RF and antenna systems, and the interfaces between electrical, mechanical, and software systems.
